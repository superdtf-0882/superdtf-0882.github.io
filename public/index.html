How I work

Product leadership is the frame for everything below. Since 2005 I have built product functions, owned platform APIs, and run enterprise architecture at business-unit scope, and in every case the question was the same: which investment in structure pays, and which should be retired. Market intimacy is how I tell the two apart: knowing the customer well enough that the architecture serves them rather than the org chart. The three domains below are where the question has produced the most.

  • Enterprise and business architecture

    Ontology-first, machine-readable blueprints on TOGAF, sized to what the organization will actually use. I have defined the enterprise data ontology for the same platform twice, a decade apart (inContact 2009–2011, NICE InContact 2021–2023), which is the test of whether an architecture holds.

    Outcome. West Corporation: a full TOGAF ADM cycle at business-unit scope; release velocity quadrupled, quality defects cut 75%, governance artifacts adopted as the PMO standard. In the CV download

  • AI-native software delivery

    Refactoring the delivery lifecycle so AI is part of how the organization works, not a tool bolted on: a maturity model, a governance plane built to be constructed rather than audited, and RAG, eval and observability design as product decisions.

    Outcome. Author of the AI-Native SDLC Maturity Model. Strata, the governance plane I built, is described here.

  • Platform integration and M&A

    Consolidating acquired capability onto the core platform instead of running it in parallel, with the data ontology as the integration point. Owning the external API surface as a governed, metered product. Taking an on-premises UCaaS/CCaaS platform (Cisco, at eLoyalty/TeleTech) to multi-tenant SaaS.

    Outcome. NICE InContact: two sequential M&A integrations (Brand Embassy, MindTouch/CXone Expert); international customer base up 60%, global revenue up 20%. inContact: invented work-item routing, the abstraction that became the tipping point for NICE’s acquisition. eLoyalty/TeleTech: migrated the business unit of an $800M services firm from on-premises Cisco to multi-tenant SaaS, with the CI/CD still running.
