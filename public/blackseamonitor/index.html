Black Sea Monitor has been retired

The Black Sea Monitor was an early-warning dashboard tracking real estate, energy and consumer signals across the occupied Black Sea territories, with a digest of the day's news written each morning.

It ran from July to October 2026. Its last digest was published on 11 October 2026.

It was a proof of concept that outlived its design, so it has been stopped on purpose. Nothing here is live any more, and no data is collected.
